price confirmation. All photos are AI-refined representations of supplied
garments. Their listed colours, cuts and fabrics must remain grounded in those
references. The original static 25-item catalogue is retained during the audit.

## Image mapping

All storefront-ready images are in `public/images/maknoon-stock/` as compressed
WebP, with corresponding JPEG social share images in
`public/previews/maknoon-stock/`. Each variant uses the pattern
`<product-id>-<colour-id>.webp` and an identically named `.jpg` preview.
Group/detail gallery photos are kept alongside the variants.

## Production notes

- No fabricated prices, sizes, stock counts or accessories. Prices are null,
  and WhatsApp requests confirmation of price and availability.
- The jet-black jilbab source is *styled with a niqab*. Inclusion of the face
  veil is unconfirmed; ask the owner before promising the pieces as a set.
- The rust/black/navy/violet rack shows only upper-garment construction. Its
  full length and closure are not represented as verified.
- The earlier 25 concept entries are preserved, with photographed-stock records
  moved to the start of the collection. The owner can retire unstocked concepts
  after completing their photo audit.
- Source logos were distracting pasted rectangles or varied text overlays; new
  composites use a consistent small `MJ / MAKNOON JAMIL` mark in clear space.
- Do not describe these AI-edited editorial images as unaltered photographs.
